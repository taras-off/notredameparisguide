Who we are & how to contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, email info@touringbee.com.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name or email through this site. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically processes basic technical data (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit) to serve the site securely and reliably.

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics to understand which pages are useful. This tells us how many people read a page — not who they are.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file a website can store in your browser. We keep cookies to a minimum:

  • Essential: needed for the site to load and display correctly.
  • Analytics (optional): anonymous, aggregate measurement of page visits.
  • Affiliate: when you click a partner link (see our Affiliate Disclosure), that partner may set a cookie so any purchase you make is credited to us. This does not change the price you pay.

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Doing so will not stop you from reading the guide.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to third parties — the TouringBee app store, ticketing partners (Tiqets, GetYourGuide, Headout), Booking.com and the official Notre-Dame website. Once you leave our site, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for their content or practices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, or to object to its processing. To make a request, contact info@touringbee.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
